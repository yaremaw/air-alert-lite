# Credits

All the game's sounds except the clicks are cut from real recordings on
Wikimedia Commons. The edited clips are under the same license as their source.

Air-raid sirens (`alert.wav`, `all_clear.wav`, `ballistic_warning.wav`):
"Air Raid Alert - Ukraine during russian invasion 2022" by Lystopad, CC BY-SA 4.0.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Air_Raid_Alert_-_Ukraine_during_russian_invasion_2022.ogg

Weapons and explosions, cut by `tools/cut_sounds.py`:

- Shahed drone engine (`drone_loop.wav`): "Ukrainian f16 destroys shahed type drone"
  by the Ukrainian Air Force, CC BY 4.0.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ukrainian_f16_destroys_shahed_type_drone.webm
- Anti-aircraft gun (`gun.wav`): "Flakpanzer Gepards engage long range simulated targets"
  by Staff Sgt. Matthew Foster, U.S. Army, public domain.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flakpanzer_Gepards_engage_long_range_simulated_targets.webm
- SAM launch and cruise missile (`sam_launch.wav`, `cruise_loop.wav`): "Patriot Live Fire (563866)"
  by Anthony Sweeney, U.S. Army, public domain.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Patriot_Live_Fire_(563866).webm
- Interceptions and drone hits (`intercept.wav`, `explosion_small_a.wav`): "War sounds" by Dragout
  (freesound), CC0.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dragout_-_War_sounds_(cc0)_(freesound).ogg
- Our launches (`launch.wav`): "USS Spruance (DDG 111) Fires a Tomahawk Land Attack Missile (997803)",
  U.S. Department of Defense, public domain.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:USS_Spruance_(DDG_111)_Fires_a_Tomahawk_Land_Attack_Missile_(997803).webm
- Explosions and destroyed sites (`explosion_a.wav`, `explosion_b.wav`, `wreck.wav`):
  "Sprengung Dach Deutschlandhalle" by Sivi Steys, CC BY-SA 2.0.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sprengung_Dach_Deutschlandhalle.ogv
